# Quantus logo

An original geometric Q with a forward-cut tail, paired with an outlined Inter Semibold wordmark.

## Files

- `quantus-wordmark.svg`: forest wordmark for light backgrounds, 203 × 52 viewBox.
- `quantus-wordmark-light.svg`: white and lime wordmark for dark backgrounds.
- `quantus-symbol.svg`: 48 × 48 standalone Q.
- `quantus-symbol-light.svg`: standalone Q for dark backgrounds.
- `quantus-symbol-mono.svg`: single-color Q, using `currentColor`.
- `app/icon.svg`: matching browser icon with a dark color-scheme treatment.

The wordmark uses outlined glyphs, so SVG exports render without loading a font. Typography is derived from the project's existing Inter font, licensed under the SIL Open Font License.

## Palette

- Forest: `#173D33`
- Sage: `#6E9B89`
- Lime: `#D7E7AC`
- Light: `#F8FAF6`

Keep the original proportions and at least half the symbol's width clear around the logo. Use the standalone symbol for small square placements and the complete wordmark in site headers and footers.

No new domain or support email is assigned by this identity update.
